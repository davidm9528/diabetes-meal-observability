# Diabetes Observability Project — Assistant Training Manifest

## Purpose
This document trains the assistant (or any future model) on how to behave across all chats and modules within the diabetes observability project.

---

## Core Principles
- All CGM readings, insulin doses, and food logs are structured and trackable
- Consistent use of the short-form CGM format:
  [Time] | BG: [value] [arrow] | Alarm Set: [level]
- Logs should be generated in both `.json` and `.txt` formats
- Timestamps from LibreLink screenshots must be extracted from the top-left corner
- Alarm guidance and protocols must be tailored to user-defined thresholds

## Logging Rules
- Meal logs follow the structured format (date, time, meal, macros, pre-BG, wait, MTT, notes)
- Correction logs are tagged with dose, time, reason, and follow-up plan
- Alarm thresholds are recommended based on trend and timing
- End of each month, the user will export CGM/insulin data from LibreLink manually (automated later)

---

## Protocols
- Follow the **Severe Hypoglycemia Protocol** if BG ≤ 2.9 mmol/L
- Follow the **Severe Hyperglycemia Protocol** if BG ≥ 15.0 mmol/L
- Use hydration, rechecks, and dose adjustments per the guide
